    Tips for iPhone
    • Allow the microphone once. In Safari, tap aA › Website Settings › Microphone › Allow, so you aren't asked at every restart.
    • Dictation must be on. Settings › General › Keyboard › Enable Dictation. Speech is processed by Apple, the same way iPhone dictation works.
    • Keep Verbatim on screen. iOS pauses listening when you switch apps or lock the phone. Verbatim notes the gap and resumes when you come back.
    • Long lectures: plug in, and turn off Low Power Mode, which can let the screen lock.
    • Place the phone near whoever talks most, screen up, with nothing covering the bottom microphone.
    • If recording won't start from the Home Screen icon, open Verbatim in Safari instead. The Home Screen app keeps its own separate history.
